The seven steps before implementing a Move
1. Understand the business problem- 
Research what an experienced practitioner is actually trying to accomplish, when the Move is useful, what makes its result valuable, and what would make it fail.
Output: Business Problem & Competency Definition

2. Research and compare candidate Methods
Investigate different ways of performing the Move. Evaluate where deterministic algorithms, expert rules, LLM reasoning, probabilistic methods or human judgment are appropriate.
Output: Method Research & Selection Report

3. Design the complete Move contract
Specify applicability, preconditions, input data, expected outcomes, output schemas, completion states, failure behavior and dependencies on other Moves.
Output: Move Definition v0.2

4. Decompose into Methods, Instruments and Actions
Define how the Move performs each meaningful step, which instruments it uses, what each Action does, and where tools and LLM calls are required.
Output: Executable Design & Action Contracts

5. Define Checks and Gates
Specify how outputs will be validated and which conditions restrict research, publication, approval, external communication or execution.
Output: Check Rules & Gate Contracts

6. Design challenging Evals
Create realistic normal, missing-evidence, conflicting-evidence, boundary, changed-state and adversarial cases. Specify expected behavior and objective test oracles.
Output: Move Evaluation Suite

7. Qualify implementation readiness
Review the design for missing evidence, unresolved business policies, incomplete handlers, unclear permissions and untestable requirements.
Output: Implementation Readiness Decision
